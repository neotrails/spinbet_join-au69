About WindCloud

Our Mission

WindCloud.site exists to make wind and renewable energy easier to evaluate in the real world. We translate technical language into useful questions about property, performance, cost, reliability, and responsibility so readers can approach a project with better expectations.

What We Cover

  • Residential and small commercial wind turbines
  • Solar-wind hybrid systems and battery storage
  • Energy savings analysis and ROI calculations
  • Government incentives, tax credits, and net metering
  • Product reviews and comparisons
  • Offshore and utility-scale wind industry news

Our Editorial Standards

We build articles from manufacturer documentation, public policy sources, technical references, and practical comparisons where those sources are available. We separate estimates from guarantees, identify assumptions, and revisit guides when products, rules, or evidence change.

Independence

WindCloud.site is independently owned and operated. We have no financial relationships with turbine manufacturers or energy utilities that influence our editorial coverage. Our affiliate disclosures are always clear and honest.

The Team

We're a small team of writers, researchers, and clean energy enthusiasts who believe the transition to renewable power starts at home. If you have a question, a story to share, or want to work with us, we'd love to hear from you.
